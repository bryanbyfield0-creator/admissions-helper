Download a copy before you clear the browser, switch computers, or change devices. Keep backups somewhere private. Imported files stay on this device only.

Privacy

  • This app runs only in your browser. There is no account and no server of ours that receives referrals.
  • Records are stored in this browser’s local storage. Clearing site data, using another browser, or using another device will not show the same list unless you restore a backup.
  • Uploaded referral PDFs are read on this device so the text can be suggested into the form. The PDF itself is not saved, and it is not sent anywhere.
  • This is not connected to an EHR, payer portal, or insurance eligibility system. It does not score clinical fit or estimate reimbursement.
  • Prefer initials instead of a full name when that is enough for your work.
